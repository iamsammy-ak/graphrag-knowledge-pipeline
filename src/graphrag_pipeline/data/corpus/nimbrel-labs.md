# Nimbrel Labs

Nimbrel Labs is a computer-vision startup based in Saltrene. Tobiah Renn started the company in 2015 with two classmates.

Nimbrel Labs developed the Glint SDK, a software toolkit that detects objects in camera images. Harrowgate Capital led its first funding round in 2016.

Since 2019 the startup has operated as a division of Velltrix Systems, which bought it for an undisclosed sum.
