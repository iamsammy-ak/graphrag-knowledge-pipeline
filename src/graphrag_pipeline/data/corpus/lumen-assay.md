# Lumen Assay

Lumen Assay is a diagnostics company headquartered in Vey Harbour. Hugo Fenwick, the chief executive of Lumen Assay, previously ran a hospital laboratory.

Lumen Assay makes the Lumen Q3, a bench-top blood analyser. In 2022 Lumen Assay partnered with Saltwind Bio to test Corallin in clinical trials.
