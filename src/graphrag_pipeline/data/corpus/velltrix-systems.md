# Velltrix Systems

Velltrix Systems is a robotics and sensing company headquartered in Quorvale. Mara Okafor founded Velltrix Systems in 2011, and she has been its chief executive since then.

The company employs about 900 people. Its engineering centre sits next to the campus of the Quorvale Institute of Technology, and many of its early hires came from that university.

In 2019 Velltrix Systems acquired Nimbrel Labs, a computer-vision startup. After the deal, Tobiah Renn became the chief technology officer of Velltrix.
